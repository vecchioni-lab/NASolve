__FRAME_NOTE__

Source and frame notes are supplied by the calling workflow. This display does not authenticate coefficient generation, frame correspondence or scientific acceptance. Model and map selections remain independent; comparing them does not establish that they belong together.

Contour bands are intersections with planes 1.1 Å apart; outlines follow the surface silhouette. A clipped or absent contour does not establish absent density. Optional fading uses distance to the displayed atoms: full strength within 1.5 Å, smoothly reduced to 15% beyond 3.5 Å. It never changes the map. Each map retains its own normalization; this is not an absolute electron-density comparison.
