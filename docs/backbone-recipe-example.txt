# Illustrative future backbone recipe
#
# This file documents the intended human-facing style for future reviewed
# custom-backbone recipes. NASolve does not execute arbitrary custom recipes yet.

Backbone recipe: standard phosphodiester example

Internal residue:
- connect previous O3' to this P
- connect this O3' to next P
- keep P, OP1, OP2
- remove OP3

5' terminal phosphate:
- connect this P to this O5'
- keep P, OP1, OP2, OP3
- do not require an incoming O3'-P connection

5' hydroxyl terminus:
- do not add P, OP1, OP2, or OP3

Notes:
- Pairing and stacking geometry are separate from this backbone recipe.
- A future GNA/PNA/TNA recipe should name the actual atoms to connect/delete.
- Do not infer a non-standard recipe from residue names or atom proximity alone.
